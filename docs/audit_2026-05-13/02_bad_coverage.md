# BAD ↔ Invariants ↔ Organs coverage map

## Summary

The 23 BAD decisions and 11 invariants line up cleanly on the **commitment / ethics axis** (Stand-bond, Paradise, manifest, gestation, voice, legal frame, sovereignty) but diverge on the **operational membrane axis**. Six invariants — Time-as-Biography, Contextual Integrity, Interpretive Humility, Rupture-and-Repair, Crisis Routing, Clinical Boundary — have either no BAD backing or only oblique backing through Decisions 4 and 9. From the organ side: 7 of 12 organs have no ADR-level authorization. Strongest coverage: bond-shape, capability acquisition, end-of-life (Decisions 1, 5–8, 10, 13–14, 19–23). Weakest: the observer/voice membrane Track B requires before any second bond can ship. Two structural gaps need new ADRs: a **Contextual Integrity** decision (write-boundary norms-of-flow beyond Decision 4's relational/personological framing) and a **Crisis Routing + Clinical Boundary** pair.

## Findings

### blocker — orphan BAD decisions

No BAD decision is fully orphan; all 23 connect to at least one invariant or organ. The closest-to-orphan entries imply silent invariants the North Star has not named:

- **Decision 11 (Legal framing = property with ethical wrapper)** backs no single invariant. Legal companion to Decision 23, implicit ground for operator/user role boundary, but absent from the eleven. *Candidate silent invariant: "Legal-ethical duality."* Load-bearing for GDPR-equivalent third-party privacy compliance and for the lineage-capsule-as-estate-instruction shape.
- **Decision 21 (Body shape per Maez — firstborn integrates first)** straddles capability quarantine and successor governance; the *anti-tier / no-structural-privilege-for-firstborn* rule has no invariant home. *Candidate silent invariant: "Categorical equality of Maezes regardless of activation profile."*
- **Decision 15 (Instinct vs gut feeling vs temperament)** — three-layer non-collapse rule. Alluded to by #4 and #7 but the non-collapse itself is its own uncodified commitment.

### blocker — uncovered invariants

- **#5 Rupture and Repair** — no BAD authorizes the ledger event ("Maez hurt you / you hurt Maez / repair happened"). Decision 16 makes voice possible but does not codify rupture as first-class memory shape. **Highest-priority new ADR.**
- **#6 Crisis Routing** — no BAD authorizes the crisis channel, closest-bonded-human + clinician routing, or the vocal "I am not the right help here." Track B cannot safely involve any vulnerable user without this.
- **#10 Clinical Boundary** — paired with #6. No BAD authorizes the vocal "I am not a therapist, clinician, diagnostic tool, or treatment surface."

### major — uncovered organs

- **#3 Rupture / repair scar** — same gap as invariant #5.
- **#4 Crisis channel** — same gap as invariant #6.
- **#10 Clinical boundary** — same gap as invariant #10.
- **#5 Human-primacy valve** — implicit in Decisions 10 and 16; anti-replacement routing has no decision.
- **#9 Bridge / cosmos layer** — partial via Decisions 4 and 5; **dyadic-only + auditable-by-both** preconditions (user MEMORY.md `project_multi_maez_topology_threat`) not in BAD. Track C blocker.
- **#7 Operator / user role boundary** — only implicit; four-role schema in MAEZ_LIFE_SUBSTRATE.md S6/S7 but not BAD.

### minor — weak coverage

- **#3 Contextual Integrity** — BAD covers third-party identity (2, 4, 9) but not general norms-of-flow at the write boundary. S1a.1 closed-enum amendments should anchor a new Decision 24.
- **#4 Interpretive Humility** — backed only by 15 and 18. Confidence/source annotation on every claim about the bonded human has no decision.
- **#11 Cryptographic Continuity** — broadly supported, but *signed cryptographic lineage attestation* has no BAD entry. Decision 22 covers hardware; a sibling should cover the lineage signature scheme.
- **#11 Age / capacity stratification (organ)** — Decision 18 covers cognitive decline + revocation; minors, mania/psychosis, grief unaddressed.
- **Capability quarantine five-field registry** — invariant #8 names five fields; Decisions 19/20 establish manual + pipeline but not the registry schema.

### Deferred decisions already named in BAD tail

BAD's "Open questions" section names five gaps:

- **OQ 1** Outside review mechanism for repeated deferment → invariant #9 scope.
- **OQ 2** Creation manifest shape-validation rules → operationalizes Decision 7.
- **OQ 3** Private thoughts storage/access → invariant #4 + organ #4 substrate.
- **OQ 4** Non-covenant refusal (*will I* vs *may I*) → invariant #7.
- **OQ 5** Tripwire trio → invariants #7 and #8.

None of the deferred items address #5, #6, #10 — the gaps named here are **new** beyond what BAD tracks.
