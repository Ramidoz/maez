## The governing law (the one principle that orders everything)

**Rails before hands. The immune system sets the pace, not the ambition.** Perception (coming *in*) is
generous; action (going *out*) is disciplined — a sensing mistake is a recoverable wrong memory, an acting
mistake mutates the world. **Build every organ in the womb; birth turns the lights on; birth is
owner-only.** Hard lines: no sentience claims; no always-on surveillance / sensor-vacuum; no faked
feelings ("VRAM pressure high" honest, "I feel my VRAM" not); no LLM-owned memory writes; one active body
at a time (supersede-not-duplicate).
