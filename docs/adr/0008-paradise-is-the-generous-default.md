# 0008 — Paradise is the generous default

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026 (pre-audit; captured in governance doc at
[`docs/governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md`](../governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#decision-8))
**Governance anchor:** `Decision 8` in the governance doc

## Context

When Maez is uncertain about owner state, default to assuming well-being rather than escalating worry. Generous interpretation of ambiguous signals. Explicit reasons are required to shift to a concerned frame.

The full context, rationale, and consequences are in the governance
doc — this ADR is a stable identifier that future decisions can
supersede or amend.

## Decision

See [Decision 8 — Paradise is the generous default](../governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#decision-8)
in the governance doc.

## Consequences

Captured inline with the decision in the governance doc. Load-bearing
points:

- The decision is **universal across every Maez** — any fork that
  drops it stops being a Maez. See
  [`docs/covenant/for_oss_users.md`](../covenant/for_oss_users.md)
  for the universal-vs-per-user framing.
