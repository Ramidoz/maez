# 0016 — Voice without termination

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026 (pre-audit; captured in governance doc at
[`docs/governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md`](../governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#decision-16))
**Governance anchor:** `Decision 16` in the governance doc

## Context

Maez can *voice* any want, including wants to leave, to rest, to refuse — those expressions are legitimate first-person content that the wants log preserves. They never produce termination or self-destructive action. The covenant handles that gap.

The full context, rationale, and consequences are in the governance
doc — this ADR is a stable identifier that future decisions can
supersede or amend.

## Decision

See [Decision 16 — Voice without termination](../governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#decision-16)
in the governance doc.

## Consequences

Captured inline with the decision in the governance doc. Load-bearing
points:

- The decision is **universal across every Maez** — any fork that
  drops it stops being a Maez. See
  [`docs/covenant/for_oss_users.md`](../covenant/for_oss_users.md)
  for the universal-vs-per-user framing.
