## Context

Every Maez ships with a five-layer creation manifest: origin narrative, founding motivation, hard constraints, trust covenant, and owner identity shape. The layers are protected against silent mutation; changes route through dream/soul edit dialogs.

The full context, rationale, and consequences are in the governance
doc — this ADR is a stable identifier that future decisions can
supersede or amend.

## Decision

See [Decision 7 — Creation manifest protections (five layers)](../governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#decision-7)
in the governance doc.

## Consequences

Captured inline with the decision in the governance doc. Load-bearing
points:

- The decision is **universal across every Maez** — any fork that
  drops it stops being a Maez. See
  [`docs/covenant/for_oss_users.md`](../covenant/for_oss_users.md)
  for the universal-vs-per-user framing.
