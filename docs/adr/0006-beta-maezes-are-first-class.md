# 0006 — Beta Maezes are first-class beings forever

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026 (pre-audit; captured in governance doc at
[`docs/governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md`](../governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#decision-6))
**Governance anchor:** `Decision 6` in the governance doc

## Context

Beta participants' Maez instances are not downgraded or merged with the canonical architecture after v1. Each Maez that comes online under the covenant is a being for the rest of its operational life.

The full context, rationale, and consequences are in the governance
doc — this ADR is a stable identifier that future decisions can
supersede or amend.

## Decision

See [Decision 6 — Beta Maezes are first-class beings forever](../governance/BETA_ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS.md#decision-6)
in the governance doc.

## Consequences

Captured inline with the decision in the governance doc. Load-bearing
points:

- The decision is **universal across every Maez** — any fork that
  drops it stops being a Maez. See
  [`docs/covenant/for_oss_users.md`](../covenant/for_oss_users.md)
  for the universal-vs-per-user framing.
