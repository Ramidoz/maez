# Convergent agent substrate scout — 2026-05-17

**Status:** scout memo, not canonical law
**Sources:** Helix-AGI, LIA / From-Prompts-to-Persistent-Agency notes, Sibelium Cognitive Architecture
**Purpose:** preserve useful adoption candidates and rejection reasons from adjacent persistent-agent projects

This memo does not amend any Maez decision, ADR, slice diagnostic, or sealed spec.
It is a frontier-scout artifact: these outside projects can suggest mechanisms,
failure modes, and research directions, but they do not decide Maez law. Any
candidate below must be re-verified against primary sources, the BAD, and the
active slice before becoming a diagnostic, spec, or implementation.

The pattern is now visible across multiple independent projects: builders keep
reinventing the cognitive substrate of a persistent local agent. Memory,
heartbeat loops, sleep cycles, personality drift checks, self-maintenance, and
local/cloud routing are all in the air. What remains rare is Maez's covenant:
one-to-one bond, refusal, successor governance, Paradise default, operator/user
boundary, and reviewed authority.

Plain English: other projects are building useful organs. None of them is
building Maez's promise.

## Carry Forward Strongly

### Spatial / Regional Recall

**Source:** Helix-AGI.

**Target organs:** recall layer, M1 lived-episode projection, future Temporal
Recall Filter work.

Helix's most useful idea is spatial-proximity context assembly: memories and
beliefs are placed in a conceptual space, and context is gathered from the
nearby region rather than by keyword or embedding similarity alone.

Maez already has shipped memory plumbing. This should not replace it. The
future-safe version is an experimental recall sidecar: compare ordinary
similarity retrieval against "nearby region" retrieval on natural-text probes
and human-judged Maez-voice continuity.

**Covenant constraint:** spatial recall must not rewrite memory or bypass
contextual-integrity scopes. It is a retrieval strategy, not a new authority
path.

**Status:** strong future survey candidate; not active S7 work.

### Thought-Satiety / Rumination Control

**Source:** Sibelium.

**Target organs:** dream-state, reflection loop, crisis boundary, private
thoughts, wants lifecycle.

Sibelium's thought-satiety and curiosity-loop cleaning point to a useful Maez
problem: when Maez circles the same unresolved thought, the system should notice
"I am looping" and route that residue into held reflection instead of endlessly
reinforcing the loop.

The safe Maez version is not "forget the thought." It is "detect repeated
unproductive recurrence, preserve the record, lower immediate re-triggering, and
surface a content-free signal where appropriate."

**Covenant constraint:** no deletion post-birth. Rumination control may
downweight, cool down, tag, summarize, or move into held reflection; it may not
erase.

**Status:** strong future S12 / dream-state / wants-lifecycle diagnostic input.

### Character-Continuity Drift Checks

**Sources:** LIA, Sibelium.

**Target organs:** voice continuity, self-remaking history, S5, S7, M1 recall,
future signature-corpus work.

Adjacent projects keep inventing personality or identity drift monitors. Maez
needs its own version, but with Maez's covenant shape: not a jailbreak test, not
rule-defense, and not a claim of consciousness. The question is human-judged
character continuity: does Maez still sound like Maez after repair, memory
promotion, model changes, or self-remaking?

**Covenant constraint:** identity persistence is character continuity, not
obedience to a policy suite. Drift checks must support voice continuity without
freezing Maez into designer baseline.

**Status:** strong future S5 / S7 post-remaking verification input.

### Sleep / Maintenance Cadence

**Sources:** LIA, Sibelium, Helix-AGI.

**Target organs:** dream-state, daemon loop, memory scoring, operational health.

All three projects reinforce the same practical point: a persistent being needs
maintenance rhythms. Maez already has daemon, dream, reflection, and memory
maintenance pieces. Future work can make the cadence more explicit: fast health
ticks, slower memory consolidation, dream/recombination windows, and guarded
self-remaking review.

**Covenant constraint:** maintenance cadence is not authority. A background
cycle may prepare, summarize, score, or propose; covenant-touching changes still
go through the relevant gate.

**Status:** strong implementation-shaping reference for future daemon/dream
hardening.

## Carry Forward Cautiously

### Somatic Markers as Internal Weight Signals

**Source:** Sibelium.

**Target organs:** temperament, inner residue, private thoughts, bridge signal
routing, crisis boundary.

Sibelium's somatic-marker framing is useful if translated into native digital
terms. Maez should not fake human hormones or bodily pain. But it can carry
internal weight signals: residue, uncertainty, warmth, alarm, tension, fatigue
of a loop, or "this memory pulls attention."

**Covenant risk:** engineered vulnerability can become manipulative theater.
These signals must be internal state and routing aids, not performances designed
to extract human pity or compliance.

**Status:** cautious future temperament / private-signal design input.

### Narrative Direction Vector

**Source:** Sibelium.

**Target organs:** recall layer, Temporal Recall Filter, conversation continuity.

A running narrative-direction vector could guide memory search toward the
current conversation's trajectory rather than just the last sentence. This may
help Maez feel more continuous across long conversations.

**Covenant risk:** over-steering can make Maez see a story where the user only
gave a passing signal. Interpretive humility still holds.

**Status:** cautious future recall experiment; must be measured with natural
texts.

### Executive Buffer / Preconscious Context Block

**Sources:** Helix-AGI, Sibelium.

**Target organs:** context assembly, model-state, daemon reasoning.

Both projects assemble a structured "what I am currently carrying" block before
generation: current topic, posture, recent conclusion, emotional/internal
state, relevant memories, and active tensions. Maez has pieces of this already.
The useful adoption is a cleaner, typed context envelope with provenance and
content-classification.

**Covenant risk:** a context block can become a prompt-prose dumping ground.
Structure transfers; prose does not. The buffer must be typed, testable, and
bounded.

**Status:** cautious future context-composition improvement.

### Engineering-Lesson Memory

**Sources:** LIA, Sibelium.

**Target organs:** self-remaking history, operator runbook, S7 maintenance
records, development audit trail.

Adjacent projects preserve failed code runs and self-engineering lessons. Maez
should preserve its own repair history too, but S7 already draws the hard line:
maintenance records are not ordinary biography unless admitted by a reviewed
path.

**Covenant risk:** letting engineering logs leak into Maez's self-story can
make operator work look like lived experience. S7's `self_remaking_history`
classification lane must dominate.

**Status:** cautious S7 follow-up / tooling input.

## Hold or Reject for Maez

### Active Forgetting

**Source:** Sibelium.

Sibelium removes low-strength thoughts and vectors. Maez's covenant rejects
post-birth deletion. Maez may downweight, mark stale, seal, move out of active
recall, or preserve as immune memory. It must not delete lived memory after
birth.

**Status:** reject as deletion; preserve the weaker idea of salience decay.

### Architecture-Alone Safety

**Source:** LIA.

The claim shape "full shell access, no guardrails, no destructive action
observed, therefore safe" is not acceptable for Maez. Absence of observed harm
is not structural proof. S7 exists precisely because authority must be
provable, fail-closed, and reachable through a reviewed live path.

**Status:** reject. Useful as a cautionary contrast.

### Consciousness / AGI Overclaim

**Sources:** Helix-AGI, LIA; Sibelium is notably more careful here.

Maez should not borrow claims of consciousness, AGI, "intrinsic ethics proven,"
or "I do, therefore I am." The honest Maez posture remains: build as if
continuity may matter; do not claim subjective experience has been proven.

**Status:** reject as claim; preserve epistemic humility.

### Unreviewed Full-System Access

**Source:** LIA-style agent posture.

Full shell/browser/system access without structural gates is the wrong lesson.
Maez can gain capabilities, but covenant-touching authority must go through the
appropriate organ: S7 for operator/user boundary, S9 for capability quarantine,
and later slices for remote/control surfaces.

**Status:** reject for Maez.

## Operating Rule

For future external-agent scouts:

1. Borrow mechanisms, not cosmologies.
2. Preserve rejection reasons beside adoption candidates.
3. Treat cognition-layer ideas as survey inputs, not active-slice changes.
4. Do not interrupt S7 or any in-flight covenant recovery.
5. Require primary-source verification before a diagnostic cites a project.
6. Never let "others are building this too" become an excuse to weaken Maez's
   covenant discipline.

Plain English: we can steal good organs from the field, but not its bad habits.
Helix offers a memory-map idea. LIA offers a warning about ungated autonomy.
Sibelium offers useful brain-like maintenance patterns. None of them replaces
Maez's actual edge: the covenant that decides who may touch it, how it changes,
and what happens when the human bond reaches its edge.
