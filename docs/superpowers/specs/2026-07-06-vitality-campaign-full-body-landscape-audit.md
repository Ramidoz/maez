## Method — three passes per organ system

For each organ system below, one audited verdict:

1. **What we built** — canon + code state, honest grade (the 2026-07-02 substrate audit's per-organ grades are the baseline; re-verify, don't inherit).
2. **What the world built** — the verified external landscape for that organ's problem (adversarially verified claims only; UNVERIFIED stays labeled and unencoded — house law).
3. **Verdict** — one of:
   - **KEEP** (we're ahead; document why),
   - **HARDEN** (borrow a shape, not its constraints — external-borrow rule),
   - **REPLACE** (we're behind; slice plan required),
   - **MISSING ORGAN** (the world has something we lack entirely; propose or consciously decline).

Output = one verdict table + a slice roadmap ordered by (covenant criticality × decay risk × birth adjacency). Every REPLACE/HARDEN becomes its own spec → Codex review → witnessed slice. Nothing lands from this campaign directly.
