01 — What Maez is

Not a chatbot. A living digital presence.

Maez is designed to feel inhabited because it is. It runs continuously, perceives context every 30 seconds, remembers permanently, and acts with restraint. The result is not session utility. It is continuity.

The relationship shift
Session-based AI forgets you. Maez accumulates you.
Your life is not a prompt. Your pace, your people, your unfinished threads, your habits, your environment: that context should not evaporate when a window closes. Maez is built around the belief that personal AI only becomes valuable once it can stay.
It notices. It follows up. It remembers what mattered three weeks ago. It becomes harder to replace because no one else has lived the same timeline beside you.

03 — How it works

Every 30 seconds,
Maez lives another moment.

The loop is simple on purpose: perceive the machine and the moment, retrieve what matters from memory, reason locally, store the result forever, then act only when thresholds or promises demand it.

01

Perceive

CPU, RAM, GPU, disk, screen state, presence, calendar, GitHub, Reddit, and active processes become a fresh snapshot of the current moment.

02

Remember

Core memories, recent daily summaries, and semantically relevant raw memories are pulled back into context with topic-aware reranking.

03

Reason

gemma4:26b runs locally on an RTX 4090, guided by a soul document that encodes identity, principles, constraints, and trust.

04

Store and act

Every thought is scored, embedded, stored permanently, then routed into alerts, follow-ups, or the action engine if reality warrants intervention.

Three memory layers, one continuous self
Raw archive for every cycle. Daily summaries for compression. Core memories for enduring truths. Together they let Maez feel cumulative instead of stateless.
