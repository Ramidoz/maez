13. Cycle prompt signals-manifest grounding (commit 19cde77) — THE BIG ONE
    Observed: the daemon's reasoning prompt said "You have full
    visibility into the system state AND screen activity above"
    even when screen_perception had failed. Every cycle the LLM
    filled the empty slot with confabulated activity narration —
    "rohit is at his desk", "working on X", "just stepped away" —
    and stored it to raw memory. Thousands of confabulated entries
    accumulated over weeks of silent-signal operation.

    Fix: dynamically build a SIGNALS PRESENT / SIGNALS ABSENT
    manifest per cycle based on which sources actually have data.
    HARD GROUNDING RULES in the prompt: if screen obs absent, DO
    NOT claim what the owner is doing. If presence absent, DO NOT
    claim presence transitions. Silence is correct behavior when
    a signal is absent.

    First cycle response after the fix: "The system is quiet. CPU
    is low at 4.3%, RAM is healthy, and disk usage is stable...
    No anomalies detected." Zero activity fabrication. Closes the
    biggest single class of Maez fabrication.
