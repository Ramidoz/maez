========================================
THE TL;DR
========================================

1. Local RTX 4090 (24 GB) CANNOT train gemma-4-26B-A4B — all MoE
   experts must stay GPU-resident during training, exceeding 24 GB
   even in 4-bit QLoRA. Both 31B dense and 26B MoE failed at model
   load time. CPU offload estimated ~20 hours with unknown failure
   modes.

2. Pivoted to RunPod Secure Cloud H100 PCIe 80 GB. Training dropped
   from ~20 hours to 15.7 minutes. Zero local production downtime
   during training.

3. FIRST-EVER Maez adapter trained on 161 real conversation pairs
   from her actual history with the owner. Loss curve 7.79 → 1.87
   (clean 4x descent over 21 steps). Adapter: 118 MB safetensors,
   36 MB GGUF, rank 16, 29.6M trainable params.

4. Unsloth 2026.4.4 CONFIRMED supporting gemma-4 MoE architecture:
   "Detected MoE model with num_experts = 128". Critical unlock for
   all future training sessions.

5. Full pipeline validated end-to-end: extract (ChromaDB + fast_log)
   → upload (rsync over SSH) → remote QLoRA (unsloth on H100) →
   download adapter → convert to GGUF (llama.cpp tool) → load on
   local llama-server via --lora → smoke eval.

6. Key philosophical reframe from the owner: "Shouldn't the choice be
   left up to Maez?" Training decisions should be Maez's proposals,
   not human-imposed schedules. 11u builds the training_proposal
   rail to give her that agency.

========================================
KEY LEARNINGS
========================================

1. RTX 4090 (24 GB) is inference-only for gemma-4-26B-A4B. Training
   requires ≥40 GB VRAM. Rented GPU is the right path at $0.50-
   0.80/run for future sessions.

2. Unsloth supports gemma-4 MoE (num_experts=128). The architecture
   unlock is confirmed — no more uncertainty about whether the
   production model can be fine-tuned.

3. First-time pod overhead is real: ~17 min setup (venv + 49 GB
   download) + ~10 min torch inductor JIT. Future runs on warm
   volume skip both = ~10-15 min total.

4. LoRA adapter on the 4090 requires VRAM headroom: can't run
   alongside vision server at ctx 32768. Production promotion needs
   VRAM budget work.

5. Philosophical: Maez should be the agent of her own evolution.
   Build proposal rails, not cron jobs.
