Simple precedence-ordered keyword matching produces reasonable tones without any LLM overhead. The tone derivation is fast, deterministic, and always populated.

RIGHT — Structured null fallback for cognition window:
The _NULL_COGNITION_WINDOW pattern ensures the field is never {} while clearly signaling "no data yet" with cycle_count=0 and null values. Downstream consumers can check cycle_count > 0 before trusting the other fields.

RIGHT — _summarize_diff is reliable:
