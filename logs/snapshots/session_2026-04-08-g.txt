WHAT WENT WRONG / RIGHT:

RIGHT — Atomic write:
Capsule uses tempfile.mkstemp + os.fsync + os.replace. A crash
during write cannot leave a partial file. Verified: capsule is
either complete or absent.

RIGHT — Startup order:
Continuity loads BEFORE the greeting/startup-message logic.
The `if not self._continuity_active: send_dev(startup_msg)` gate
correctly suppressed the restart announcement during orientation.

RIGHT — Orientation expiry:
After exactly 5 cycles, the capsule was archived and deleted.
Normal operation resumed. No lingering [CONTINUITY] block after
the window closed.

RIGHT — Three write paths work:
- Periodic: build_capsule("periodic") → write_capsule()
- Pre-restart: pre_restart_write(skip_llm=True) in evolution_engine
- Graceful shutdown: continuity_shutdown() in daemon stop()

MINOR — cog_metadata scope:
The initial implementation used `'cog_metadata' in dir()` to
check if the variable existed in the local scope. This worked
but was fragile. Fixed to use `self._last_cog_metadata` stored
after each scoring pass.
